  • Serving: press once to throw the ball up, again to hit it.
  • Hold the button to hit harder; hold longer still and it goes up as a lob.
  • Steer while you swing to place the ball: sideways puts it across the court, forward drives it deep, back drops it short.
  • You have two serves. Miss both and the point is gone.
High scores

One player opens a match and passes on the code; the other enters it. Whoever opens the match plays blue, the second player red.

Needs a relay: run npm start yourself, or put the address of a Cloudflare Worker in src/config.js. See the README.
